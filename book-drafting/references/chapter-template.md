# Chapter Template

Use this as a fallback, not as a mandatory structure. Prefer the repository's outline and editorial rules.

```markdown
# Chapter title

Opening: connect the reader's problem or current knowledge to this chapter.

## What the reader will learn

State the concrete outcome in prose or a short list when the book's style permits it.

## Main concept or task

Explain why it matters before introducing detailed steps or terminology.

### Procedure or example

Present one action or idea at a time. Explain the expected result and how to recognize it.

## Points to check

Summarize cautions, constraints, or verification steps without adding new claims.

## Chapter recap

Reinforce the reader outcome and bridge to the next chapter when appropriate.
```

Avoid formulaic headings when the outline calls for a narrative structure. Do not add a recap, learning-objectives list, or bridge merely to fill the template.
