# Editing Checklist

## Chapter Contract

- Is the chapter's purpose clear and fulfilled?
- Is every major section required by the approved outline or reader outcome?
- Does the text avoid assuming knowledge not yet introduced?

## Structure and Continuity

- Does information appear before it is needed?
- Can repeated explanations be consolidated or assigned to one chapter?
- Do the opening and ending connect naturally to adjacent chapters?
- Are procedures complete, ordered, and followed by a way to verify the result?

## Meaning and Evidence

- Did the revision preserve conditions, exceptions, cautions, and uncertainty?
- Do citations still support the sentence or paragraph where they appear?
- Are potentially obsolete facts clearly identified for fact-checking?
- Did an example accidentally become a general rule?

## Language and Notation

- Is terminology consistent with the canonical terminology file?
- Are sentences and paragraphs focused on one main idea?
- Are headings informative and parallel where appropriate?
- Does the manuscript use only supported Markdown and Pandoc conventions?
- Does the voice match `$olein-writing-style` without becoming repetitive or overly polished?

## Final Comparison

- What meaning changed between the original and revision?
- Was any useful example, qualification, source, or authorial nuance lost?
- Are editorial comments and unresolved questions kept outside publishable prose?
